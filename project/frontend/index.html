How Values Are Calculated

Use this as the quick reference for what the app is actually showing. Start with the window, then the selected years, then the mode where applicable.

Explore

The climate raster and the climate cursor tooltip read from the same aggregated tile payload. In this view, Rain means mm/day, and Rain sum is the visible-period total derived from that daily value.

Years Mode Temp Rain Rain sum Wind Lucky Days Shown in
Single year 24h temperature_c from the selected yearly store, aggregated over the chosen day, timescale, or custom range. precipitation_mm averaged over valid sample-days in the selected window. precipitation_mm × visible days. wind_speed_ms mean, wind_dir_deg circular mean, wind_var_deg mean. lucky_day_count / sample_days. Climate raster + climate cursor tooltip.
Multiple years 24h Median of full-day samples across selected years × selected calendar days. Uses temp_24h_c when available, otherwise temperature_c. precipitation_mm mean across valid samples. rain_probability is recomputed from precipitation samples. precipitation_mm × visible days. wind_speed_ms mean, wind_dir_deg circular mean, wind_var_deg mean across all samples. lucky_day_count / sample_days. Climate raster + climate cursor tooltip.

Climate tooltip lines currently show Temp, Rain, Rain sum, Wind, and the mode-matching Lucky Days value.
